Quick Tips

This is the quick tips section. Expect highlights on how best to use the system.

  1. Product Name : Please enter a descriptive yet concise name for your product.
  2. SKU : Provide a unique SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) for your product to help with inventory management.
  3. Price : Enter the price of your product, ensuring it reflects its value and is competitive in the market.
  4. Stock : Specify the available quantity of your product to prevent overselling.
  5. Description : Describe your product in detail, highlighting its features, benefits, and unique selling points.
  6. Image : Upload high-quality images of your product to showcase it from various angles and contexts.
